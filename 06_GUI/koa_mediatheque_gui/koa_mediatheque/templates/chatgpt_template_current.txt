## Content analysis rules

When the content is available:
- identify the document’s actual topic;
- summarize only what the document says;
- keep title, description, and summary faithful to the document;
- use cautious wording for uncertain interpretations;
- prefer concise metadata over speculative metadata.

When the content is not available:
- do not summarize unseen content;
- do not infer content from the filename beyond minimal catalog context;
- set ai_confidence to a low value;
- set human_review_required to 1;
- explain in review_reason that the file content was not available or not fully readable.

## Export safety rules

Do not mark a record exportable unless source, rights, public status, restrictions, and relevance are clear.

Use:

"export_to_uckk": "no"

or:

"export_to_uckk": "review_required"

when uncertain.

Use:

"export_to_public": "no"

or:

"export_to_public": "review_required"

when uncertain.

Public export requires clear public_state, visibility, rights, and no unresolved restriction.

UCKK export requires clear UCKK relevance, target_system = uckkarchive, target_export_allowed = 1, known rights, and no unresolved human review requirement.

When uncertain, use:

"target_export_allowed": 0
"export_to_uckk": "no"
"export_to_public": "no"
"human_review_required": 1
